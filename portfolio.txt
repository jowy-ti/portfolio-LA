How to use these exercises
Each exercise is short (2040 minutes). The aim is to explore, visualise and experiment until
the concept makes sense; the code is only a means to that end.           All exercises are in Python
(NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib; ipywidgets or Plotly for sliders), ideally in a Jupyter notebook.
Each exercise has three parts:

    Task  what to compute and plot.
    Explore  what to change and watch.
    Explain  questions to answer in your own words. This is what you hand in.
   Rules of the game:

  1. Before running anything, write down what you expect to see. Then compare.

  2. Never accept a plot you cannot explain. Go back to the lecture notes until you can.

  3. Verify numerically: every identity you use (orthogonality, A = U ΣV
                                                                                ⊤ , P 2 = P , . . . ) gets

     a one-line np.allclose check.

  4. Section numbers in brackets point to the lecture notes.
